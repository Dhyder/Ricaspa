Our Spa Journey

At Rica Spa, our journey began with a simple vision — to create a sanctuary where wellness, beauty, and relaxation come together in perfect harmony. We believe self-care is essential, not optional. Every treatment, product, and ritual is thoughtfully chosen to help you reconnect with yourself, unwind deeply, and leave feeling renewed, confident, and radiant.

From everyday relaxation to special pampering moments, Rica Spa is where your wellness story continues.

"At Rica Spa, every client experience matters. Our goal is not just relaxation, but genuine healing — physically and emotionally. Seeing clients leave lighter, calmer, and happier is the most rewarding part of my work."

Rica Spa., Senior Wellness Therapist
